Privacy Policy

Video Upload Scheduler uses Google sign-in only to manage its owner's own YouTube channel: uploading videos and setting their titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails and schedules.

It does not collect, store or share data from anyone else. Nothing is sold or passed on.

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in their Google Account settings, under Security, Third-party apps and services.
